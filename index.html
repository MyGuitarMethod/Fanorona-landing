A SIMPLE MOVE. A DEEPER GAME.

Read the board.
Find your opening.

Move along connected lines. Capture by approaching your opponent—or by withdrawing. Link your captures into a chain, and turn a small opening into a very different game.

01 / APPROACH

Close the distance.

Move towards an opposing line to capture its connected pieces.

02 / WITHDRAW

Make room. Take ground.

Move away from an opposing line to capture behind you.

03 / CHAIN

Keep the idea going.

Continue capturing with the same piece, changing direction as the position unfolds.

THE PEOPLE ACROSS THE TABLE

Meet 19 characters, each with their own portrait, story, and place in the campaign. Win your matches, earn their boards and sets, and face the surprises of a rematch.

YOUR FIRST MATCH HAS A GUIDE

Learn with Finn.
Then find your own way.

New to Fanorona? Let the person across the table teach you. Finn’s guided match introduces movement, captures, chains, and the restrictions that make each decision matter.

Already know your way around the board? Skip the lesson and play. When you want to practise, Custom Game lets you choose an unlocked opponent, a board, and your light and dark pieces independently.
